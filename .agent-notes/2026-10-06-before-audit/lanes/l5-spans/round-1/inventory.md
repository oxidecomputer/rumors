## Models and oracles

1. **Vector model** (`vle`, `vcmp`, `vjoin`, `vmeet`, `vproject`,
   `oracle_place`, `oracle_dominance`, `oracle_precedence`; `bdaec1dc`).
   - What: versions as height vectors over a shared leaf partition;
     pointwise order and lattice.
   - Oracle independence: it shares no code with production. `oracle_*`
     transcribe the public docs' definitions.
   - Reach: it is the oracle for everything below.
2. **`Layout` and builders** (`tree_by_layout`, `party_by_layout`,
   `version_of`, `party_of`; `bdaec1dc`, with `Layout` from `19080507`).
   - What: build canonical production values from vectors on a uniform grid,
     a spine with chosen turns, a random full binary shape, or an organic
     overlay.
   - Reach: production trees up to depth 40 (spines) with up to 41 leaves,
     against `arb_oracle_version`'s depth 4 and 16 leaves.
3. **`census`** (`bdaec1dc`).
   - What: exact `Coverage` by census over the sublattice generated by
     `{lo, hi, clamp(bound)}`. The doc comment carries the exactness argument.
   - Calibration: `l5_census_is_exact_against_a_finer_universe` checks the
     census against all 81 functions on a finer 4-cell grid (heights 0..2),
     for random down and up conjunctions, at 200 cases. It passes.
   - Reach: exact coverage beyond the committed 9-version grid.

## Generators

4. **`arb_world`** (`bdaec1dc`).
   - What: uniform grids of 1 to 16 cells (64 for the deep variant) over
     seven value alphabets. These are small integers; `W + {0,1,2}`;
     `{0, 1, W, W+1}`; `{0, W, 2W}`; `{W-1, W, W+1}`; `{0, 1, W-1, W^2}`,
     with `W` from `2^31` to `2^1000`.
   - Reach (measured): all nine placements, with shares of `Before` at 9.6%,
     `Concurrent(Start)` at 12.6%, and `Concurrent(End)` at 12.3%. The
     committed walk population gives 1.5%, 2.4%, and 2.2%. The alphabets put
     near-equal wide heights side by side; the committed generators reach
     those only by chance or in one constructed membership family (not
     counted).
5. **`extended`, `with_bumps`, `span_pairs`** (`bdaec1dc`, `5cec22e4`).
   - What: close a pool under pairwise join and meet, add single-cell bumps up
     and down (pairwise-concurrent antichains), and add the bottom vector and
     the bump hulls. Spans are every ordered pair.
   - Reach (measured): antichains of 2 to 5 stored holes in 81 of 251 down
     cases and 89 of 251 up cases. Committed families hold at most two holes.
6. **`arb_shaped_world`** (`19080507`).
   - What: spines of 1 to 40 internal nodes with random turns, and random
     shapes of depth up to 10.
   - Reach (measured): 338 of 771 versions at depth 17 to 64. The committed
     walk differentials (`arb_oracle_version`) have none deeper than 4.
     `verdict_matrix` reaches deep registry shapes, but not for exact
     multi-hole coverage.
7. **`arb_organic_world`** (`19080507`).
   - What: op-trace populations (`optrace::world_strategy`) mapped onto the
     overlay of every version and party shape. An assertion round-trips each
     organic version through the overlay.
   - Reach (measured): depth at most 5 and at most 16 leaves. This is
     shallow, but it is the only exact-coverage check on organic shapes; the
     committed suite checks only soundness there.
8. **Clause generators and folds** (`arb_n`, `arb_d`, `arb_u`,
   `conjoin_neutral`, `conjoin_down`, `conjoin_up`; `bdaec1dc`).
   - What: every public atom, negation, `or_concurrent`, strict form,
     `delta`, and `toward`. Each item joins on the left or right as `shape`
     picks, so both operand orders of every typed `&` cell occur.

## Why none is briefed for landing

The repo's bar for a new instrument is a constructible failure that no
existing instrument catches. I could not construct one. Every non-equivalent
mutant I injected, including ones aimed at multi-bound settle order, wide
numeric discards, and the clamp refinement, also failed the committed suite.
The reach above is real and measured, but I cannot yet name a failure that
only this reach exposes. Two candidates for the owner's triage, if reach
alone is wanted:

- the exhaustive cube (item 15), which follows the repo's "exhaust the
  finite domain" convention at a 7.7 s debug cost
- the shaped spines (item 6). `tests/verdict_matrix.rs` already reaches deep
  registry families for placement, single-hole membership, and neutral or
  point coverage. The shaped worlds add exact multi-hole coverage at depth up
  to 40.
