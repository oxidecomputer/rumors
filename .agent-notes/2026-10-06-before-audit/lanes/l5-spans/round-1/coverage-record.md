Revision: `explore/l5-spans` @ `2874e0c3` (base `58285ca5`, production code
unmodified; the branch adds only `crates/before/src/testing/audit_l5.rs` and

## What was established, and how

The oracle is a vector model: every version in a world is a vector of
heights over one shared leaf partition. The partition is a uniform dyadic
grid, a spine or random full binary shape (`Layout`), or the overlay of an
organic op-trace population's shapes. The causal order is the pointwise
order; join, meet, and projection are pointwise max, min, and masking. It
shares no code with production comparison, lattice, walk, or query code.
Production values are built from vectors through the tree oracle's
normalizing constructor and `from_oracle_version`.

Coverage is checked against `census`: an exact census over the sublattice
generated by the segment's endpoints and every bound clamped into the
segment. Its exactness argument is in its doc comment. It was checked
against a full finer universe (all 81 functions on 4 cells, heights 0..2) by
`l5_census_is_exact_against_a_finer_universe`, which passes.

## Reach compared with the committed generators (measured)

From `l5_reach_committed` and the stats probes, 256 cases each:

- **Shape.** The committed walk differentials draw `arb_oracle_version`
  trees: of 768 operands, all had at most 16 leaves and depth at most 4 (380
  at depth 3 with at most 8 leaves). The shaped worlds reach depth 16 to 64
  with at most 64 leaves (338 of 771 versions at depth 17 to 64).
- **Exact coverage.** Committed exact-coverage checks run only on the
  9-version two-party grid (2 cells, heights 0..2). On arbitrary versions,
  coverage is checked for soundness only (`coverage_bounds_membership`) or
  against the per-bound composed fold (`filter_coverage_matches_the_composed_sweeps`),
  not against an exact census. The committed `[meet, join]` spans with a hole
  at `c` read `Partial` in 211 of 256 cases. The explore census checks exact
  coverage on 16-cell grids, spines to depth 40, random shapes, organic
  populations, and the exhaustive cube. Verdict mix (`l5_queries`, 256
  cases): down Empty 7725, Full 1074, Partial 1651; up Empty 7674, Full 983,
  Partial 1793; neutral Empty 7140, Full 2056, Partial 1254.
- **Placement mix.** Committed population: After 802, At(Start) 434,
  At(End) 434, Between 350, At(Both) 344, Concurrent(Both) 250,
  Concurrent(Start) 66, Concurrent(End) 62, Before 43. Grid worlds:
  Concurrent(Both) 57692, Between 35993, Concurrent(Start) 33076,
  Concurrent(End) 32482, After 25277, Before 25277, At(End) 20344,
  At(Start) 19209, At(Both) 13716. As shares: `Before` 1.5% committed versus
  9.6% in the grid worlds; `Concurrent(Start)` 2.4% versus 12.6%;
  `Concurrent(End)` 2.2% versus 12.3%.
- **Antichain holes.** `l5_antichain_queries` stores 2 to 5 pairwise
  concurrent holes in about a third of cases per polarity (81 of 251 down,
  89 of 251 up). The committed `down_queries` and
  `up_queries` families hold at most two holes, and
  `coverage_is_exact_on_the_two_party_grid` builds at most two-hole
  antichains.
- **Numeric.** Alphabets put near-equal wide heights (`W - 1, W, W + 1` with
  `W` up to `2^1000`, and `W^2` up to `2^2000`) in adjacent cells and across
  versions, so private differences are built, discarded, and rebuilt on wide
  values. `arb_magnitude` reaches wide values, but near-equal wide pairs only
  by chance. `filters_materialize_a_difference_only_when_needed` constructs
  them deliberately for membership and the composed fold, not for exact
  coverage. Not measured as a count.

## Blind spots that remain

- Organic populations under the op-trace cap are shallow: depth at most 5,
  overlay at most 16 leaves in the 128-case sample. Deep organic shapes are
  reached only through the synthetic spines.
- Census cost limits coverage checks to about 41 leaves; very wide shapes
  (hundreds of leaves) get verdict checks, not coverage checks.
- No wasm32 execution: `wasm32-pins` has no span or query check. I found no
  lane-specific pointer-width mechanism (see the observations file), so I
  did not add one.
- Heap: I measured scan and touch counters, not peak heap. The closed
  multi-hole auxiliary-space fix was re-attacked by reading the code (see the
  observations file), not by a new heap meter.

## How to resume

Every instrument is in `crates/before/src/testing/audit_l5.rs` on
`explore/l5-spans`. Re-run the long commands above after any change to
`version/place.rs`, `version/place/filter.rs`, `causally/`, or `span/`. To
recalibrate, reintroduce `L5_MUT` switches through a `crate::l5_mut(name)`
helper (the pattern is described above) and restore afterwards.
